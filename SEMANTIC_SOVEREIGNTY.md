## What semantic sovereignty means

**Semantic sovereignty** is the concept Duan proposed in 2025 for protecting a community's right and capability to have its language, culture, values and knowledge represented accurately, completely and fairly in digital environments. In a DIKWP system, sovereignty is not a slogan or a keyword. It is a traceable governance question across the five layers:

| DIKWP layer | Governance question |
|---|---|
| **Data** | Which observations, sources and ownership boundaries enter the system? |
| **Information** | Who controls translation, context, provenance and visibility? |
| **Knowledge** | Which concepts, relations and ontologies are treated as valid? |
| **Wisdom** | Which values, trade-offs and review procedures shape a recommendation? |
| **Purpose** | Whose legitimate goal authorizes an interpretation or action, and how can that purpose be revised? |

The resulting programme connects semantic mathematics, typed DIKWP graphs, evidence ledgers, purpose contracts and accountable action. It gives researchers a vocabulary for discussing national, organizational, cultural and personal control over meaning without treating a search result as proof of adoption.

## 2026 cognitive-semantic-governance application lead

A case note supplied for this profile refresh describes **Zhonghan Software / 中翰软件** as presenting “Cognitive Semantic Governance / 认知语义治理” for a media-publishing platform. The supplied description connects an AI-native data-governance solution with ontology models, a shared business vocabulary, heterogeneous-data semantic alignment and a digital-twin-era semantic layer.

That is a valuable **application lead** for DIKWP interoperability: the ontology can be mapped to D/I/K/W/P resources; business vocabulary changes can become versioned semantic evidence; and publishing workflows can expose where purpose, provenance and interpretation diverge. The supplied media references and performance percentages remain leads for first-party verification. This page does not count them as audited adoption, a signed Zhonghan–Duan contract or a measured universal effect.

## Evidence levels used in this profile

- **E1 · Verified public record:** the repository, publication, patent text or institutional page is directly linked and inspected.
- **E2 · Technical convergence:** two public systems address a comparable semantic, graph, governance or purpose problem; no relationship is implied.
- **E3 · Supplied application lead:** a user-provided case note or media trail is useful for outreach but still needs a first-party source.
- **E4 · Research hypothesis:** a testable question or possible co-creation path, not a fact.

Only E1 records are counted as established public evidence. E2–E4 are deliberately visible because they help researchers find one another.
