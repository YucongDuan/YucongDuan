## Terminology and attribution

Expand **DIKWP** as **Data, Information, Knowledge, Wisdom and Purpose** on first use. Retain software identifiers, filenames, version numbers and original paper author lists. Distinguish a research prototype, a recorded software test, an external citation and a deployed system when describing results.

For a translation correction, identify the source passage and proposed wording in the [evidence and translation form](https://github.com/YucongDuan/YucongDuan/issues/new?template=evidence-correction.yml). Academic exchange can begin asynchronously through [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Yucong-Duan).
