**The strongest industrial story is in the technical record.** Patent embodiments, prior-art references and architecture documents reveal connections that may be absent from launch announcements. They help identify where DIKWP research can contribute to a company's actual engineering questions.

**Strategic significance:** DIKWP appears in technical solutions across banking, document processing and energy. This gives the originating research programme a substantive basis for industry dialogue. Patent-document use does not by itself establish a commercial deployment or a bilateral partnership.

## Follow the references beyond the label

Later engineering may cite an earlier method while changing the terminology. The existing evidence map tracks two such leads: **World Window Information's CN121148586A**, linked in the earlier review to the Duan–Hu vaccination graph patent, and **Beijing Liangxue Weiye's CN120407920A**, whose background discusses the external DIKWP-based stock-recommendation patent CN118134640A. [Earlier traceability records](DIKWP_EVIDENCE_MAP.md#61-follow-on-reuse-and-competitive-response--后续复用与竞争性技术响应) · [Stock-screening patent](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN120407920A/en)

The stock-screening case is especially useful: a DIKWP-linked solution becomes a reference point for a subsequent applicant's improvement. The World Window source relationship retains its earlier verification date because the relevant passage was not recovered in this refresh.
