**The strongest industrial story is in the technical record.** Patent embodiments, prior-art references and architecture documents reveal connections that may be absent from launch announcements. They help identify where DIKWP research can contribute to a company's actual engineering questions.

This gives the originating research programme a substantive basis for industry dialogue. Patent-document use does not by itself establish a commercial deployment or a bilateral partnership.
