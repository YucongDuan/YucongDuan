# DIKWP: research that travels across disciplines

[中文](IMPACT.zh-CN.md) · [Profile](README.md) · [Industry connections](INDUSTRY_CONNECTIONS.md) · [Publications](PUBLICATIONS.md)

DIKWP's public footprint spans international standardization, enterprise patent documents, independent scholarship, professional publishing and open engineering. These connections reveal the reach of a research programme organized around a central question: **how do meaning and purpose become computational?**

## International standardization

**ISO/IEC TR 25005-2:2025** provides a particularly concrete connection. Clause 6.2 uses DIKWPA in smart-city use-case analysis; clause 7.1 uses it as a foundation for the data value chain. The official bibliography, reference 22, cites Wu and Duan's 2024 DIKWP uncertainty paper. [Official catalogue](https://www.iso.org/standard/86195.html) · [Text preview, §§6.2 and 7.1](https://standards.iteh.ai/catalog/standards/iso/31c3610b-9085-481b-8081-6f8850ec1dbb/iso-iec-tr-25005-2-2025) · [Official bibliography](https://www.iso.org/obp/ui?_escaped_fragment_=iso:std:iso-iec:tr:25005:-2:ed-1:v1:en)

This is methodological use in a published international **Technical Report**. It gives researchers and city-data practitioners a shared reference for discussing Purpose, Action and data value.

## Academic foundations

Hainan University records the team's **third prize in the 2020 Wu Wenjun AI Science and Technology Awards** for DIKW graph extension and modelling. It also records Duan's work on intent-driven learning, resource integration and technology transfer. [University profile](https://en.hainanu.edu.cn/info/1151/2981.htm)

For the **CAAI 2024 white-paper series**, Duan led the team coordinating the first two chapters of *Artificial Intelligence Foundations: Selected Topics*: DIKWP foundations and artificial consciousness. [University authorship record](https://cs.hainanu.edu.cn/info/1035/9040.htm) · [CAAI publication](https://www.caai.cn/index.php?s=/home/article/detail/id/4375.html)

The book programme extends this work into semantic mathematics, consciousness, active medicine and economics. [Selected books and bibliographic records →](PUBLICATIONS.md)
