## Why this map exists / 为什么建立这张地图

DIKWP-related influence now appears across international standardization, commercial product language, industrial practice, external patents, academic work, student projects, cloud-platform architecture, and open-source research systems. Those signals are not equivalent. This map makes the evidence level and claim boundary visible so that impact can be communicated strongly **without collapsing citation, similarity, implementation, licensing, partnership, and endorsement into one claim**.

DIKWP 的影响已经出现在国际标准、商业产品表述、产业实践、外部专利、独立学术研究、学生项目、云平台架构和开源系统中，但这些证据的强度与含义并不相同。本地图的目的，是在彰显影响力的同时，明确区分“引用、采用、结构趋同、产品实现、授权合作与机构背书”。

## Evidence ladder / 证据分级

| Code | Evidence class | What it can establish | What it does not establish by itself |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S4** | Standard-body methodological adoption | A published standard or technical report uses DIKWP/DIKWPA as part of its method or theoretical foundation. | Endorsement of every DIKWP product, patent, organization, or claim. |
| **U3** | Direct named public use | A product, company page, patent, project, or application publicly names DIKWP as a framework, model, graph, or technical component. | Proven authorization, licence, derivation, production scale, or commercial success. |
| **U2** | Independent scholarly uptake | Researchers outside the originating author team cite, adapt, evaluate, or apply Purpose-DIKWP. | Product deployment or institutional adoption by the authors' affiliations. |
| **O2** | Source-team open or peer-reviewed implementation | DIKWP is converted into runnable software, evaluated engineering architecture, or institutionally recorded project work. | Independent replication, production deployment, or external adoption. |
| **P3** | Independent patent diffusion | An external applicant's patent text substantively uses DIKWP as a model, graph, resource structure, or functional method. | Grant, validity, commercialization, freedom to operate, licence, or non-infringement. |
| **C2** | Strong structural convergence | Public architecture combines DIKW with Purpose/intention, resource integration, execution planning, or comparable DIKWP-adjacent structure. | Proof that the structure was derived from, licensed from, or formally named DIKWP. |
| **C1** | Adjacent DIKW extension | Public work extends DIKW toward goals, action, agents, conversational intent, or ethical decision-making, but the full DIKWP fingerprint is incomplete. | DIKWP adoption, derivation, avoidance, or infringement. |
| **D1** | Public dissemination | A third-party platform, media outlet, or developer community carries DIKWP-related material. | Platform-level adoption or endorsement. |
| **F0** | Platform or collaboration fit | A technology or organization has a plausible use case for DIKWP but no direct public implementation evidence has been found. | Any claim of present use. |

## 4. Non-naming structural convergence / 未直接使用 DIKWP 名称的结构趋同

This section records public architectures that do not directly use the name **Purpose-DIKWP** but expose unusually close structural fingerprints. It does **not** infer that an organization intentionally avoided a name, derived its work from Yucong Duan, or implemented a DIKWP patent. Motivation requires direct evidence; structural similarity can be documented independently.

本节记录未直接使用 **Purpose-DIKWP** 名称、但公开呈现高度相近技术指纹的架构。这里可以核验“结构相似度”，不能在缺少内部材料、引用链或授权记录时推断某机构“故意避名”、来源于段玉聪研究或实施了某项 DIKWP 专利。

### 4.1 Alibaba Cloud: DIKW + user intention + execution

Alibaba Cloud provides one of the clearest publicly inspectable structures adjacent to DIKWP's extension of DIKW with Purpose/intention:

- The [2023 ApsaraDB / 瑶池 article](https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1363914) places DIKW in the data-platform model and states that the platform should understand data content, clarify user intention, and integrate data resources.
- A [2024 Alibaba Cloud article](https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1480735) repeats the DIKW, user-intention, and data-resource-integration formulation.
- The [2025 DMS Data Agent article](https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1686127) explicitly maps its architecture to DIKW, performs intention clarification, and generates an adjustable intention-execution plan.
- The [DMS+X article](https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1634358), marked as official content, combines DIKW, intent recognition, data, and AI in a one-stop platform narrative.
- An [Alibaba Cloud observability article](https://developer.aliyun.com/article/1649517) explicitly describes applying the DIKW pyramid to observability.
- The [user-provided evidence synthesis](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2040560733583364350) preserves the first-figure context in which user intention surrounds DIKW.

**Assessment: C2 — strong structural convergence.** The public material establishes a DIKW + intention + resource-integration + intention-execution architecture. No verified public attribution, licence, derivation, or formal Alibaba statement of Purpose-DIKWP adoption has been found.

### 4.2 AWS: DIKW + Intelligence + intent-driven agent execution

AWS's current [Prescriptive Guidance for a Semantic Layer for Agentic AI](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/semantic-layer-agentic-ai-ontology-reasoning-virtual-knowledge-graph/introduction.html) states that DIKW inspired its layered architecture. It then:

- extends DIKW with an **Intelligence** tier for continuous self-assessment, ontology evolution, truth maintenance, and agent context graphs;
- realizes Wisdom in an Orchestration Layer that makes decisions, answers questions, takes actions, and invokes human review;
- uses context graphs containing inferred **user intent**;
- implements [intent classification and intent-driven orchestration](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/semantic-layer-agentic-ai-ontology-reasoning-virtual-knowledge-graph/orchestration.html), routing a question through execution planning, virtual knowledge graphs, GraphRAG, symbolic reasoning, and response synthesis;
- returns evidence, citations, confidence, and optional reasoning steps with end-to-end provenance.

**Assessment: C2 — strong structural convergence.** This is an official, executable reference architecture combining DIKW, semantic resources, inferred intent, execution planning, action, continuous learning, auditability, and human governance. AWS extends DIKW with “Intelligence,” not with a named Purpose layer, and the guidance does not attribute the architecture to DIKWP.

### 4.3 TM Forum and China Mobile: DIKW maturity + intent-driven closed loops

The official [TM Forum IG1252 Autonomous Network Levels Evaluation Methodology](https://www.tmforum.org/resources/introductory-guide/ig1252-autonomous-network-levels-evaluation-methodology-v1-2-0/) defines a technical maturity model by referring to DIKW and progressing from manual implementation through AI-assisted knowledge to self-evolution. A [publicly accessible 2022 industry whitepaper](https://13115299.s21i.faiusr.com/61/1/ABUIABA9GAAg7e6koQYojPOlkwU.pdf) records the same China Mobile DIKW-based maturity approach and, across its reference architectures, adds:

- user, business, service, and resource closed loops;
- user-intent management and intent extraction;
- business intent cascading to service and resource layers;
- Intent APIs that translate user requirements into system instructions;
- instruction execution, feedback, autonomous remediation, and evolving business goals.

The whitepaper lists contributors from China Mobile, China Telecom, China Unicom, Huawei, Ericsson, Nokia, NTT, Vodafone, Verizon, ZTE, TM Forum, and many other industry organizations.

**Assessment: C2 — strong ecosystem-level structural convergence.** The same public industry framework brings DIKW-based cognitive maturity together with multi-layer intent, execution, and feedback. The document is collaborative; it does not establish that every listed contributor implemented every element, nor does it name or attribute Purpose-DIKWP.

### Why these records matter / 为什么这些材料重要

Taken together, these records show that the industry's design frontier is moving toward a recurring structure: **typed semantic layers → contextual or graph knowledge → explicit intent/goal → execution plan or action → feedback, provenance, and revision**. That recurring structure is materially adjacent to DIKWP. The evidence supports a strong opportunity for attribution research, interoperability work, citation outreach, and technical comparison—not a retrospective claim of ownership over every similar system.

这些材料共同说明，产业前沿正在反复出现一条相似路径：**语义分层 → 上下文或图谱知识 → 显式意图/目标 → 执行计划或行动 → 反馈、来源追踪与修订**。这与 DIKWP 的技术思想高度相邻，足以支持进一步的出处核查、兼容性研究、引用沟通与技术比对，但不能反向推定所有相似系统都源自 DIKWP。

## 7. Exclusions and ambiguity controls / 排除项与歧义控制

The map deliberately excludes or reclassifies:

- **acronym collisions:** for example, a 2026 SAGE Open manufacturing paper uses DIKWP to mean **Data–Information–Knowledge–Wisdom–Practice**, not Yucong Duan's **Purpose**-centred DIKWP; it is therefore not counted as direct adoption;
- generic DIKW pyramids with no Purpose/intention or substantive DIKWP link;
- search-result snippets without a stable public record;
- platform-hosted contributor posts as evidence of platform-level adoption;
- patent search hits where DIKWP appears only incidentally;
- future collaboration fit presented as current use;
- unverified claims of licence, partnership, endorsement, commercialization, or infringement.

## 8. How to add or correct evidence / 如何补充或纠正证据

Please open a GitHub issue or submit a pull request with:

1. the canonical public URL;
2. publication date and responsible entity;
3. the exact DIKWP relationship: citation, adaptation, use, product, patent, standard, structural convergence, or platform fit;
4. a short extract or section locator;
5. attribution and licensing status, if known;
6. a suggested evidence grade and the boundary it does not cross.

欢迎企业、机构、研究者和开源项目主动补充来源、实现范围、许可状态、可复现实例与效果证据。最有价值的影响力材料不是模糊口号，而是可定位、可复核、可运行、可引用、可纠正的公开记录。
