# DIKWP-SemanticJustice-OS

[Repository / 仓库](https://github.com/YucongDuan/DIKWP-SemanticJustice-OS) · [Research area / 研究领域](../directory/dikwp-foundations.md) · [All projects / 全部项目](../REPOSITORY_DIRECTORY.md)

An open-source, offline-first semantic-space justice scaffold. It transforms legal norms, facts, evidence, and stakeholder narratives into DIKWP semantic ledgers, then produces a non-adjudicative explanation report for human legal review.
