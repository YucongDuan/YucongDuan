# RELATIONLAB-54

[Repository / 仓库](https://github.com/YucongDuan/RELATIONLAB-54) · [Research area / 研究领域](../directory/dikwp-foundations.md) · [All projects / 全部项目](../REPOSITORY_DIRECTORY.md)

DIKWP RELATIONLAB-54 converts the 54 unresolved problem units of *A New Physical History of the Universe* into bilingual, executable, source-bound research capsules. It preserves the book's conceptual architecture while changing the public object from a finished answer to a living comparison process
