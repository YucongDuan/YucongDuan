# COSMOWEAVE

[Repository / 仓库](https://github.com/YucongDuan/COSMOWEAVE) · [Research area / 研究领域](../directory/dikwp-foundations.md) · [All projects / 全部项目](../REPOSITORY_DIRECTORY.md)

COSMOWEAVE is an offline research system for studying a core hypothesis: worlds are not defined by raw information volume, but by the selective alignment between physical propagation modes and causally effective information flow.
