# DIKWP-OpenLocus

[Repository / 仓库](https://github.com/YucongDuan/DIKWP-OpenLocus) · [Research area / 研究领域](../directory/dikwp-foundations.md) · [All projects / 全部项目](../REPOSITORY_DIRECTORY.md)

OpenLocus removes static `human/person/good/evil/civilized` classes from the decision kernel. It represents time-indexed DIKWP content, continuity claims, event-specific conduct, protection triggers, responsibility debt, repair and re-entry.
