## Data-quality and claim boundary / 数据质量与表达边界

1. The DOCX filename says “119 AI-related patent abstracts,” but its document body contains **115 complete title–abstract pairs and 115 metadata tables**. This map uses the parsed record count and preserves the filename discrepancy.
2. The primary 139-item workbook and the rendered PDF agree on the application number for item 6: **2025113528790**. A companion workbook contains a different number for that item. The repeated primary/PDF value is used here, and the conflict remains documented.
3. The primary summary workbook omits application numbers on 17 rows; the companion filing workbook supplies identifiers for those same titles. This is reported as **122 primary + 17 companion identifiers**, not as 17 unfiled inventions.
4. A title-level item can later correspond to multiple publications, jurisdictions, continuations, grants, or legal events. Conversely, multiple source rows can belong to one legal family. Only patent-office records and current legal-status data can resolve those relationships.
5. Classification is a research-navigation aid based on titles, not a legal claim chart, valuation, freedom-to-operate opinion, or statement of validity.
